## Story

One person, one situation, in plain words. What they do and what they see.

## Acceptance

1. **A pass/fail claim a check can prove,** with what counts as passing.
2. **One behaviour per criterion.** A criterion with a judgment word (*clear*, *easy*) is the PM's to score.

## Not this

- What a reader might assume is in scope and is not.

## Why

The value test (from F103 on; `scripts/lint.sh` checks it). One line each:

- **Lives somewhere better?** Does it already live on their website or Google? Then link out instead.
- **Only locals know?** Home bakers, pop-ups, a run club with no website: our edge.
- **Gets people together?** In person. The mission; counts double.
- **Stays fresh on its own?** Dated things do; static facts go stale.
- **Who keeps it current?** Needing the solo operator or constant owner upkeep is a cost.
- **Better as more people join?** Follows, vouching, exchange.
