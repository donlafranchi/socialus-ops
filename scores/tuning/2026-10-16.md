## Is each score measuring what it should? (false reds and misses, with examples)

**score-ci-health: overall red, mostly real.**
- `ci_main_fail_pct` 19.4% red: **mixed.** 26 of 134 runs on main failed. Three on 2026-10-16 were real and self-healed within the hour: `Vitest` failed on `src/lib/migrations/manifest.test.ts` (migration 20261016150052 landed without its manifest; fixed by #817), and run 38921301005 failed the "migrations applied to production" gate by design (it said apply first). Two on 10-15 were `Browser: feature evals and screenshot matrix`. All 124 push runs are counted equally, so a same-day red-then-fixed streak counts three times. A miss: the score cannot tell "main was red for 40 minutes" from "main stayed red for a day"; time-to-green on main is the better measure.
- `scheduled_red` 1 amber (tap-speed): **real.** Run 38921267909 failed on "Explore post card → Page post [quick]", content 1228 ms, flag `slow-content`, `slowerThanBaseline`. The alternating pass/fail runs on 10-15 suggest the budget sits at the noise edge.

- `smoke_red_pct` none (1 of 4): fine as unbanded; 4 scheduled runs in 14 days is thin. A miss: it counts runs, not routes, so a red on one Page route reads the same as a red everywhere.
