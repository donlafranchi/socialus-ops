# Review a method against how we work

Written 2026-11-12 from the knowledge store (practice.review-a-method). Use with how-we-work.md.

**As of 2026-11-10** · Source: socialus-plan scenario F128, criterion 4 · Maintained by: hand (research note; companion to the pack draft; deleted at 30 days)

Use with the pack. Read only the material given and the pack: no web research beyond the pages handed over.

1. **List the method's claims,** at most 15, each one practice in plain words, with the page it came from.
2. **Place each against the pack:** *we do this* (name the pack line), *we do the opposite on purpose* (name the line and its why), *a gap* (nothing in the pack covers it), or *clashes with a philosophy* (name it). Note cost and plan tier where the material states it.
3. **Weigh against the context block first:** traffic, budget, one PM, the stack. A practice that needs scale or money we don't have is *hold for now*, with the condition that would change that.
4. **Recommend one experiment at most,** as A/B/C with a one-line trade-off each and the recommendation first, and how it would be measured.
5. **Say what was not read** and what the answer assumes.
